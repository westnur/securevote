How SecureVote works

A purpose-built voting ledger that replaces trust in intermediaries with cryptographic proof.

1

Tokenize shares

Each shareholder receives voting tokens proportional to their stake — 1 share = 1 token.

2

Open a resolution

The corporate secretary publishes a poll. Its rules are hashed and written to the chain.

3

Vote anonymously

Ballots are stored without identity. Each voter gets a private receipt to verify their vote.

4

Instant, final results

Votes are mined into blocks immediately. Any edit breaks the hash chain and is detected.

🔒

Tamper-proof

SHA-256 hash chain with proof-of-work. Changing a single vote invalidates every block after it.

🕶️

Anonymous

The ledger records the choice and its weight — never the voter's name, email or wallet.

🔍

Verifiable

Anyone can audit the public ledger; every shareholder can confirm their own ballot with a receipt.
